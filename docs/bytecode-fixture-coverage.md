# Venom/O1 bytecode fixture coverage

## What has been compared

The [101 retained fixture names](../lowering/defs/bytecode/supported-fixtures.txt)
identify 101 concrete HOL programs and corresponding Vyper sources. For each,
checked `compile_vyper` produced **exactly** the deployment and runtime bytes
independently generated by the Python compiler at [`VYPER_PIN`](../VYPER_PIN):
**101 programs, 202 byte-list equalities**. No checked-compiler `NONE` result,
byte normalization, alternative backend, or HOL-derived expected output counts
as a match. The original 23-fixture milestone is included; the `spill_join`
stress fixture was retired and is not in the corpus.

The comparison profile is experimental Venom, Prague,
`OptimizationLevel.NONE` / `PASSES_O1`, no final assembly optimization, and no
bytecode metadata. Fixture sources live in
[`python-o1-no-asm-opt-sources/`](../lowering/defs/bytecode/python-o1-no-asm-opt-sources/),
HOL programs in [`evalCompilerScript.sml`](../lowering/defs/bytecode/eval/evalCompilerScript.sml)
and the neighboring `evalCompilerSubset*` theories, and independently generated
bytes in [`python-o1-no-asm-opt/`](../lowering/defs/bytecode/python-o1-no-asm-opt/).
Examples of **tested programs**, rather than claims about every case of a
language construct:

## Frozen HOL classification boundary

The authority for **what HOL implements**, rather than which programs Python
can produce or which fixtures happen to pass, is the frozen, machine-readable
`.agent-files/tasks/evidence/TASK_089.ledger.json`. Its 1,485 stable rows
cover every datatype constructor in `syntax/vyperASTScript.sml` and the
lowering dispatch arms in nine sources under `lowering/defs/`:
`compileVyper`, `moduleLowering`, `stmtLowering`, `exprLowering`, `abiEncoder`,
`compileEnv`, `context`, `valueEncoding`, and `selectorDispatch` (`*Script.sml`).
Every row records a HOL symbol and source line, classification, recorded
shape/type boundary, pinned Python symbol, and any assigned fixture names.
The ledger and its source-review/derivation tools are **gitignored local
planning evidence**, not files in this PR; this document is their public
summary, not a replacement for the row-by-row ledger.

| Classification | Rows | Frozen meaning |
|---|---:|---|
| Supported | 506 | Existing code-producing HOL path, subject to its stated input conditions. |
| Partial | 795 | Both code-producing and rejected/unrepresentable shapes; the ledger states the dividing condition. |
| Unsupported | 184 | Explicit `INVALID`, missing dispatch, or unrepresentable inputs; not a parity claim. |

Examples of **boundaries**, not an exhaustive listing: integer widths must be
multiples of 8 from 8 to 256; `bytesM` widths must be 1–32; dynamic bytes and
arrays require valid positive bounds; structs and flags require resolved
compile-environment metadata. Default-argument selector entries, immutables,
some raw/create/selfdestruct call branches, account `Code`, and unsupported
dynamic internal-return shapes remain outside the corresponding HOL lowering
paths. The six HOL-supported denominations listed below are instead excluded
**only** from the pinned-Python elaborated-input differential target: their
HOL definitions and classifications remain frozen and unchanged. A Python
frontend rejection must not be used to silently reclassify a HOL code-producing
row as unsupported.

## What the fixtures do **not** establish

- **Complete lowering-arm coverage is unverified.** Of the 1,301 HOL
  code-producing rows, 1,295 have assigned fixture **names**. Those names
  were partly assigned by symbol-name heuristics; the check verifies that
  the names exist, **not** that a program executes its named row. They must
  not be reported as 1,295 verified execution witnesses. For example,
  `scalar_minmax_int` was originally assigned to denomination rows despite
  containing no `as_wei_value` call; the genuine Wei–KEther cases are in
  `scalar_wei_small` and `scalar_wei_large`.
- **Six known rows have no fixture and are outside the pinned-Python
  elaborated-input differential target:** HOL AST constructors `MEther`,
  `GEther`, and `TEther`, and their three `denomination_multiplier` lowering
  arms. HOL can compute 10^24, 10^27, and 10^30 for them, but pinned Python
  `vyper/builtins/functions.py::AsWeiValue.wei_denoms` accepts denominations
  only through `kether` (10^21). This exclusion does **not** remove those HOL
  constructors or reclassify their HOL code-producing paths. See the local
  TASK_089 ledger for the precise source symbols and exclusion reasons.
- The ledger's 184 unsupported rows and the unsupported shapes within its
  795 partial rows are **not claimed to work** or to match Python. The 101
  programs do not cover arbitrary inputs, every valid combination of features,
  other EVM versions/optimization profiles, or the retired `spill_join` case.
- The 202 concrete byte equalities are **finite differential evidence**, not a
  universal equivalence theorem. TASK_091's separate uncached final gate was
  not run. Unrelated pre-existing proof debt is not claimed to be resolved.

For the compiler boundary and checked source-level correspondences, see
[compiler-definition-parity.md](compiler-definition-parity.md). Full
row-by-row exercise coverage would require auditing each fixture assignment
against its actual HOL program before making a stronger claim.
